I started working on my thesis at SAC, ISRO in November 2023, at a cryosphere lab under Dr. Sushil Singh. I was there for seven months, and the experience gave me exposure to a wide spectrum of remote sensing data and introduced me to Python libraries for geospatial analysis. I was surrounded by a couple of amazing senior researchers who were always open to brainstorming with me. My time here showed me how vast and interesting the field of Earth observation is, and that I had only scratched the surface of an iceberg.
