Namaste!

I am Krutika Dave from Gujarat, India. 

My career trajectory has been interdisciplinary so far, and I enjoy that about myself. Transitioning from studying the anatomy of microorganisms to the anatomy of algorithms is not an easy journey—a challenge that keeps me intellectually engaged. I continue to grow in this realm, driven by my little curious and exploratory brain. 

I find myself fascinated by learning about various AI models and understanding the kind of remote sensing data I work with. My interest originated while working on an ecology project involving coral reef monitoring using satellite data. Later, since I have always been drawn to working on climate-change related problem statements, I chose to work on one for my master's thesis at SAC, ISRO. I am currently working in an AI lab at GalaxEye space, contributing to the development of exciting computer vision models for national sovereignty. 

Along the way, I keep building on my interests, both at work and outside of it, which continue to shape who I am. Hence, this portfolio is a work in progress.
